# PAIN-01: Gate Control - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Gate Control, a 15-minute protocol for pain modulation.

**Goal:** Use attention to modulate pain signals through the gate control mechanism.

**Mechanism:** 10 Hz alpha binaural beats with pulse cues help you direct attention to modulate pain perception.

**Process:** Three phases. Locate the sensation precisely, modulate the signal through attention, then release. This is clinical pain management.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Locate (0-5 minutes)

**00:00** Gate control protocol. Notice the sensation.

**01:00** Where is it located? Observe precisely.

**02:00** What are its qualities? Notice without judgment.

**03:00** You have control over your attention to this signal.

**04:00** Notice how observation changes the sensation.

## Phase 2: Modulate (5-12 minutes)

**05:00** Now modulate the signal. You have control.

**06:00** Notice the sensation. You interpret this signal.

**07:00** Shift your attention. Notice how sensation changes.

**08:00** You have control over the gate. Modulate the signal.

**09:00** Notice the sensation. You control your response.

**10:00** Modulate the signal. You have this control.

**11:00** Notice how you can change the signal.

## Phase 3: Release (12-15 minutes)

**12:00** Now release. You have control.

**13:00** Notice the sensation shifting. You modulated the signal.

**14:00** You have control over this gate. Notice the change.

**15:00** Protocol complete. You have control.
