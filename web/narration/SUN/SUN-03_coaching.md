# SUN-03: Small Universe - Advanced Three Dantian Orbit - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Small Universe Advanced Three Dantian Orbit, a 36-minute deep circulation practice.

**Goal:** Stabilize lower, middle, and upper dantian awareness, then integrate into full-orbit circulation.

**Breath:** Long and natural. In the full-orbit phase, use approximately 10 seconds inhale and 10 seconds exhale.

Let's begin.

---

## Phase 1: Lower Dantian Root (00:00-06:00)

**00:00** Rest attention below the navel. Feel weight and warmth in the lower dantian.

**03:00** Keep breath smooth and unforced. Store, do not push.

**06:00** Move toward middle dantian.

## Phase 2: Middle Dantian Open (06:00-13:00)

**06:00** Let awareness expand through the center of the chest.

**09:30** Keep shoulders soft and breath quiet.

**13:00** Transition upward.

## Phase 3: Upper Dantian Clarify (13:00-20:00)

**13:00** Light attention at brow-center and center of head.

**16:30** Stay relaxed. If pressure builds, drop awareness to lower dantian.

**20:00** Begin full-orbit circulation.

## Phase 4: Full Orbit (20:00-33:00)

**20:00** Inhale up spine to crown. Exhale down front body to lower dantian.

**24:00** One complete orbit per breath cycle.

**28:00** Keep continuity through all points of the orbit.

**33:00** Begin sealing.

## Phase 5: Seal (33:00-36:00)

**33:00** Let orbiting soften into stillness at lower dantian.

**34:30** Collect and store all energy in lower dantian.

**36:00** Seal complete.
