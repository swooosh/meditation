# SUN-01: Small Universe - Beginner Orbit - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Small Universe Beginner Orbit, an 18-minute foundational circulation practice.

**Goal:** Establish smooth, unforced orbit breathing and end by storing energy in the lower dantian.

**Breath:** Inhale 6 seconds up the back channel, exhale 6 seconds down the front channel. No strain.

Let's begin.

---

## Phase 1: Settle (00:00-03:00)

**00:00** Relax your shoulders, jaw, and abdomen. Rest awareness in the lower dantian.

**01:30** Let the breath lengthen naturally. Keep it smooth and quiet.

**03:00** Begin gentle orbit attention.

## Phase 2: Orbit Foundation (03:00-15:00)

**03:00** Inhale up the spine to the crown. Exhale down the front body to the lower dantian.

**06:00** Keep one complete orbit per breath cycle. No forcing upward.

**09:00** If pressure rises in the head, return awareness to lower dantian.

**12:00** Continue smooth 6-in, 6-out breathing.

**15:00** Begin closing.

## Phase 3: Seal (15:00-18:00)

**15:00** Let orbiting soften. Rest awareness in the lower dantian.

**16:30** Imagine all energy collected and stored in the lower dantian.

**18:00** Seal complete.
