## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Focused Sustained Practice, a 31-minute foundational attention training protocol.

**Goal:** Develop stable, sustained attention on a single object without distraction.

**Mechanism:** Progressive binaural beat frequencies with brown noise create optimal conditions for sustained focus, while pulse cues mark attention checkpoints.

**Process:** Three phases. Anchor your attention, deepen into sustained focus, then lock into stable concentration. This is the foundation of all attention training.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Downshift (0:00 - 7:00)

**00:00**
Find a comfortable position. Close your eyes. Begin breathing naturally through your nose.

**00:30**
Inhale for 4 counts. Exhale for 6 counts. Let the exhale be slightly longer than the inhale.

**01:30**
Notice the weight of your body. Feel gravity pulling you down. Let go of any tension.

**03:00**
Continue the breath pattern. 4 in, 6 out. Nothing to do but breathe.

**05:00**
Your nervous system is downshifting. Allow it.

---

## Phase 2: Body Scan (7:00 - 12:00)

**07:00**
Bring attention to your feet. Notice any sensation there. Don't change anything, just observe.

**08:00**
Move awareness up through your legs. Knees. Thighs. Hips.

**09:00**
Scan through your torso. Belly. Chest. Shoulders.

**10:00**
Notice your arms. Hands. Fingers.

**11:00**
Scan your neck. Face. Crown of head. You've mapped your entire body.

---

## Phase 3: Attentional Lock (12:00 - 20:00)

**12:00**
Now narrow your attention. Pick a single point of focus. The breath at your nostrils. Or the rise and fall of your chest.

**13:00**
When your mind wanders, gently return to that point. This is the practice.

**15:00**
The soundfield will support your focus. Let it hold you.

**17:00**
You're building attentional stability. Stay with the anchor point.

---

## Phase 4: State Shift (20:00 - 28:00)

**20:00**
Allow your attention to soften. Let the boundaries blur slightly.

**22:00**
You may notice shifts in perception. Time may feel different. This is normal.

**24:00**
Don't grasp at anything. Just observe what arises.

**26:00**
The soundfield is guiding you deeper. Trust the process.

---

## Phase 5: Reintegration (28:00 - 31:00)

**28:00**
Begin to bring awareness back to your body. Feel your breath.

**29:00**
Notice the room around you. The sounds. The temperature.

**30:00**
When you're ready, gently open your eyes. Take your time.

**30:30**
You've completed the protocol. Well done.
