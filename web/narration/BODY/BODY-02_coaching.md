# BODY-02: Power Embodiment - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Power Embodiment, a 20-minute protocol for somatic empowerment.

**Goal:** Fully embody your physical power and presence.

**Mechanism:** Moderate-intensity tribal drums with sub-bass and pulse cues support active movement and power expression.

**Process:** Three phases. Awaken your power, amplify it through movement, then anchor it as your baseline state. This is embodied strength.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Awaken (0-5 minutes)

**00:00** Power embodiment. Stand tall. Feel your connection.

**00:30** Three deep breaths. You are strong.

**01:00** Notice your body from inside. Feel your power.

**01:30** Hand on heart, hand on belly. Feel your life force.

**02:00** Roll shoulders back. Feel your power expanding.

**02:30** Notice how breath deepens in your power.

**03:00** Feet rooted. Your power rises. You are strong.

**03:30** Move your arms. Power flows from your center.

**04:00** Appreciate this awakening. Your body knows its power.

**04:30** Prepare to amplify. Feel your power gathering.

## Phase 2: Amplify (5-15 minutes)

**05:00** Now amplify. Stand wider. Feel your power.

**05:30** Arms overhead. Feel your power reaching. You are strong.

**06:00** Lower with intention. Feel your power flowing. Embody this.

**06:30** Move your whole body. Let your power express.

**07:00** Feel your power in your voice. Let it be heard.

**07:30** Move hips, spine. Feel your power as fluid, alive.

**08:00** Clench fists, release. Feel your power in both.

**08:30** Step forward with purpose. Feel your power in each step.

**09:00** Pause and breathe. Feel your power as steady. Embody this.

**09:30** Expand your chest. Feel your power radiating.

**10:00** Arms in large circles. Feel your power creating space.

**10:30** Stomp gently. Feel your power connecting with earth.

**11:00** Twist torso. Feel your power as flexible.

**11:30** Reach in all directions. Feel your power extending.

**12:00** Make yourself big. Feel your power filling space.

**12:30** Hold this expansion. Feel your power as unshakeable. Embody this.

**13:00** Slow your movements. Feel your power settling.

**13:30** Hands on heart. Feel your power as love.

**14:00** Three powerful breaths. Feel your power integrating.

**14:30** Prepare to anchor. Feel your power ready.

## Phase 3: Anchor (15-20 minutes)

**15:00** Now anchor. Mountain pose. Feel your power as foundation.

**15:30** Close your eyes. Feel your power from inside.

**16:00** Breathe normally. Your power moves with breath.

**16:30** Scan your body. Feel where your power lives.

**17:00** Create a gesture for your power. Embody this symbol.

**17:30** "I am strong. I embody my power. This is who I am."

**18:00** Imagine carrying this power into your day. You are ready.

**18:30** Feel your power as a gift to the world.

**19:00** One final breath. Feel your power anchored.

**19:30** Open your eyes. You are strong. You embody your power.

**20:00** Complete. Carry this embodiment with you.
