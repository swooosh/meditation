# FSP-02: Distributed Attention - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Distributed Attention, a 25-minute protocol for training panoramic awareness.

**Goal:** Develop the capacity to hold multiple attention points simultaneously.

**Mechanism:** Spatial audio sweeps and distributed pulse cues train your attention to expand across a wider field without losing coherence.

**Process:** Three phases. Anchor in single-point focus, progressively distribute attention across multiple points, then integrate the expanded capacity.

Let's begin.
