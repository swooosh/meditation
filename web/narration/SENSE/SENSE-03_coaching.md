# SENSE-03: Synesthetic Mapping - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Synesthetic Mapping, a 20-minute protocol for cross-sensory awareness.

**Goal:** Develop the capacity to perceive connections between different sensory modalities.

**Mechanism:** Spatial sweeps and harmonic pads create a rich multi-sensory environment that encourages synesthetic perception.

**Process:** Three phases. Anchor in one sense, explore cross-sensory connections, then integrate the expanded perceptual capacity.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Anchor (0-4 minutes)

**00:00** Synesthetic mapping. Choose a sensory anchor.

**00:30** What do you notice about this sensation?

**01:00** How does it feel? Where is it?

**01:30** Notice the qualities. What do you notice?

**02:00** What other sensations emerge?

**02:30** Where is this most vivid?

**03:00** How does it feel to rest here?

**03:30** Let this anchor establish fully.

## Phase 2: Cross (4-16 minutes)

**04:00** Now invite another sense. What do you notice?

**04:30** How does it feel when senses meet?

**05:00** What emerges? Where is the meeting point?

**05:30** What do you notice about this crossing?

**06:00** What connections are forming?

**06:30** Where is the most vivid intersection?

**07:00** What do you notice about preferences?

**07:30** How does it feel when senses blend?

**08:00** What do you notice about rhythm?

**08:30** How does it feel with multiple senses?

**09:00** What do you notice about the pattern?

**09:30** Where is attention drawn?

**10:00** What do you notice when you amplify?

**10:30** How does it feel naturally?

**11:00** What surprises you?

**11:30** How does it feel when boundaries soften?

**12:00** What do you notice about emotional tone?

**12:30** How does it feel at the edges?

**13:00** What do you notice about attention?

**13:30** Where is the most aliveness?

**14:00** What do you notice about fullness?

**14:30** How does it feel immersed?

**15:00** What connections feel significant?

**15:30** How does it feel as crossing completes?

## Phase 3: Integrate (16-20 minutes)

**16:00** Now integrate. What do you notice?

**16:30** What remains most vivid?

**17:00** Where is the lasting impression?

**17:30** How does it feel to recognize this?

**18:00** What do you notice about your environment?

**18:30** How does it feel integrated?

**19:00** What do you want to remember?

**19:30** How does it feel to know this?

**20:00** Complete. Synesthetic awareness available.
