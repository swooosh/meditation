# SOC-02 - Group Resonance

## Overview
**Duration**: 30 minutes  
**Family**: SOC (Social Coherence)  
**Intensity**: Medium  
**Experience Level**: Intermediate

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: improve interpersonal timing, attunement, and co-regulation.
- Protocol-specific aim: increase connection quality without over-merging or emotional spillover.
- Best use case: dyadic or group contexts where trust, listening, and coordination matter.

## Session Methodology
Treat this as relational training: pacing, breathing, and attentional synchrony are the core skills.

### Phase Map
- Phase 1: **Attune** (0:00-10:00) - Increase interpersonal sensitivity and timing awareness.
- Phase 2: **Sync** (10:00-20:00) - Align rhythm and attention across participants.
- Phase 3: **Resonate** (20:00-30:00) - Stabilize shared coherence without over-merging.

### Audio Stack
- `noise_bed`
- `binaural`
- `harmonic_pad`
- `pulse_cues`

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
Shared timing cues and synchronized breath pacing support interpersonal entrainment and co-regulation.

In practical terms, the protocol combines:
- Repetition for state stabilization
- Gradual transitions to prevent abrupt overactivation
- Cue-based timing to guide attention, breath, and pacing

## Theory (Why This Design)
Uses interpersonal neurobiology concepts: synchronized rhythm can improve felt safety, coordination, and prosocial attunement.

This is a training protocol, not a one-off effect. Benefits usually accumulate with repeated practice.

## What Listeners Typically Notice
- Early response: timing and relational pacing feel more coherent.
- Mid-session shift: co-regulation cues are easier to notice.
- End-state: closing phase supports connection without emotional spillover.

### This Session Timeline
- Attune (0:00-10:00): increase interpersonal sensitivity and timing awareness.
- Sync (10:00-20:00): align rhythm and attention across participants.

## Usage Guidelines
- Use stereo headphones at conservative volume.
- Keep posture stable and jaw/shoulders relaxed.
- If agitation increases, reduce volume and return to natural breathing.
- Prefer regular practice (several sessions/week) over intensity spikes.
- After the session, include a short verbal check-in to consolidate interpersonal signal clarity.
