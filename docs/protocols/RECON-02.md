# RECON-02 - Grief Container — Loss Processing

## Overview
**Duration**: 35 minutes  
**Family**: RECON (Emotional Reprocessing)  
**Intensity**: Medium  
**Experience Level**: Intermediate

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: process emotional material inside a structured, regulated container.
- Protocol-specific aim: support reappraisal and integration without emotional flooding.
- Best use case: reflective sessions with journaling, therapy support, or quiet post-session integration.

## Session Methodology
Move gently and allow experience to unfold; this protocol prioritizes safety, pacing, and integration over catharsis volume.

### Phase Map
- Phase 1: **Safe Space** (0:00-7:00) - Build psychological and physiological safety before deeper work.
- Phase 2: **Allow** (7:00-20:00) - Allow experience to arise without suppression or escalation.
- Phase 3: **Resolution** (20:00-35:00) - Downshift activation and consolidate emotional processing.

### Audio Stack
- `noise_bed`
- `binaural`
- `harmonic_pad`
- `room_illusion`

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
Safety-first phase progression lowers defensive reactivity, allowing emotional material to surface and reconsolidate in a regulated state.

In practical terms, the protocol combines:
- Repetition for state stabilization
- Gradual transitions to prevent abrupt overactivation
- Cue-based timing to guide attention, breath, and pacing

## Theory (Why This Design)
Draws from reconsolidation-informed practice: memories are most malleable when reactivated in safety and paired with new regulatory cues.

This is a training protocol, not a one-off effect. Benefits usually accumulate with repeated practice.

## What Listeners Typically Notice
- Early response: protective tension softens enough for safe contact with feeling.
- Mid-session shift: memory/emotion links feel more workable and less fused.
- End-state: resolution phase restores regulation and orientation.

### This Session Timeline

- Allow (7:00-20:00): allow experience to arise without suppression or escalation.
- Resolution (20:00-35:00): downshift activation and consolidate emotional processing.

## Usage Guidelines
- Use stereo headphones at conservative volume.
- Keep posture stable and jaw/shoulders relaxed.
- If agitation increases, reduce volume and return to natural breathing.
- Prefer regular practice (several sessions/week) over intensity spikes.
- If activation becomes too strong, orient to the room, lengthen exhale, and pause before resuming.
