# CREA-02 - Divergent Thinking

## Overview
**Duration**: 20 minutes  
**Family**: CREA (Creative Emergence)  
**Intensity**: Medium  
**Experience Level**: Intermediate

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: loosen rigid thought loops and open associative bandwidth.
- Protocol-specific aim: convert cognitive stuckness into exploratory idea flow.
- Best use case: before ideation, writing, design exploration, or strategic reframing.

## Session Methodology
Use this as creative priming: permit ambiguity in early phases so novel links can emerge in the middle arc.

### Phase Map
- Phase 1: **Baseline** (0:00-5:00) - Map current state clearly before modulation.
- Phase 2: **Diverge** (5:00-15:00) - Guide a stable transition toward the protocol target state.
- Phase 3: **Integrate** (15:00-20:00) - Organize insights and restore usable baseline function.

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
Controlled novelty, subtle unpredictability, and rhythmic shifts interrupt habitual prediction loops to widen associative search.

In practical terms, the protocol combines:
- Repetition for state stabilization
- Gradual transitions to prevent abrupt overactivation
- Cue-based timing to guide attention, breath, and pacing

## Theory (Why This Design)
Uses predictive-processing and incubation concepts: mild uncertainty and rhythm variation can promote novel recombination.

This is a training protocol, not a one-off effect. Benefits usually accumulate with repeated practice.

## What Listeners Typically Notice
- Early response: habitual thought loops loosen early.
- Mid-session shift: mid-session novelty and cross-linking of ideas increase.
- End-state: ending phase consolidates usable insights.
