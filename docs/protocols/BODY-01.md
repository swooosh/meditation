# BODY-01 - Grounded Power — Embodied Presence

## Overview
**Duration**: 15 minutes  
**Family**: BODY (Somatic Power)  
**Intensity**: Medium  
**Experience Level**: Beginner to Intermediate

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: increase grounded embodiment, posture coherence, and somatic confidence.
- Protocol-specific aim: convert mental activation into stable, actionable physical presence.
- Best use case: before leadership moments, training sessions, or difficult conversations.

## Session Methodology
Prioritize felt sense over conceptual analysis; let posture, breath, and weight distribution do the work.

### Phase Map
- Phase 1: **Root** (0:00-5:00) - Establish ground connection
- Phase 2: **Build** (5:00-12:00) - Accumulate power
- Phase 3: **Hold** (12:00-15:00) - Stable embodied presence

### Audio Stack
- `noise_bed`
- `sub_power`
- `body_cues`
- `room_illusion`

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
Low-frequency grounding and phase-based activation cues increase body ownership and postural readiness.

In practical terms, the protocol combines:
- Repetition for state stabilization
- Gradual transitions to prevent abrupt overactivation
- Cue-based timing to guide attention, breath, and pacing

## Theory (Why This Design)
Grounded in embodied cognition: posture, interoception, and rhythmic input shape perceived agency and confidence.

This is a training protocol, not a one-off effect. Benefits usually accumulate with repeated practice.

## What Listeners Typically Notice
- Early response: posture and breath feel heavier, steadier, more grounded.
- Mid-session shift: somatic confidence rises with reduced upper-body tension.
- End-state: finish phase leaves usable embodied presence.

### This Session Timeline
- Root (0:00-5:00): establish ground connection.
- Build (5:00-12:00): accumulate power.
- Hold (12:00-15:00): stable embodied presence.

## Usage Guidelines
- Use stereo headphones at conservative volume.
- Keep posture stable and jaw/shoulders relaxed.
- If agitation increases, reduce volume and return to natural breathing.
- Prefer regular practice (several sessions/week) over intensity spikes.
- Stand and take 30-60 seconds of deliberate posture integration before re-entering activity.
