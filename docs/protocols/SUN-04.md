# SUN-04 - Small Universe - 13-Point Dantian Orbit

## Overview
**Duration**: 30 minutes  
**Family**: SUN (Small Universe)  
**Intensity**: Medium  
**Experience Level**: Intermediate

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: train precise point-to-point internal circulation through a full 13-point orbit.
- Protocol-specific aim: coordinate inhale-receive and exhale-transfer without forcing.
- Best use case: seated, standing, or lying internal practice when you can maintain smooth attention.

## Session Methodology
Practice is continuous and calm:
- Inhale: receive energy at the current point.
- Exhale: move energy to the next point.
- Keep tongue lightly on the roof of the mouth and body relaxed.

### Phase Map
- Phase 1: **Settle and Connect** (0:00-3:00) - Relax, connect front/back channels, gather at lower dantian
- Phase 2: **Guided 13-Point Orbit** (3:00-18:00) - Follow the complete point sequence with coached pacing
- Phase 3: **Self Practice** (18:00-27:00) - Continue sequence independently with smooth breath
- Phase 4: **Seal** (27:00-30:00) - Store in lower dantian and close practice

1. Lower dantian

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
The protocol uses repeated breath-linked transfers to stabilize attention, improve internal mapping continuity, and reinforce lower-dantian closure.

## Theory (Why This Design)
This is a deterministic, cue-driven form of Small Universe practice: a consistent sequence and breath cycle reduce cognitive load so subtle interoceptive tracking can become clearer over repetitions.

## What Listeners Typically Notice
- Early response: easier settling and more stable lower-dantian focus.
- Mid-session shift: smoother transitions between orbit points.
- End-state: stronger sense of centeredness after the seal phase.

## Usage Guidelines
- Use stereo headphones at conservative volume.
- Keep breath natural and unstrained.
- If head pressure or agitation appears, return attention to lower dantian.
- Finish the full seal phase before ending.
