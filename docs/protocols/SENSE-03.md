# SENSE-03 - Synesthetic Mapping

## Overview
**Duration**: 20 minutes  
**Family**: SENSE (Sensory Precision)  
**Intensity**: Medium

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: increase perceptual resolution while staying regulated and oriented.
- Protocol-specific aim: refine exteroceptive/interoceptive signal discrimination.
- Best use case: sensory training, body-awareness work, or pre-performance tuning.

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
Attention-gating cues and low-load entrainment increase salience of subtle external and internal signals.

In practical terms, the protocol combines:
- Repetition for state stabilization
- Gradual transitions to prevent abrupt overactivation
- Cue-based timing to guide attention, breath, and pacing

## Theory (Why This Design)
Uses perceptual-learning logic: repeated directed attention improves resolution and discrimination of weak signals.

This is a training protocol, not a one-off effect. Benefits usually accumulate with repeated practice.

- Mid-session shift: perceptual granularity improves without forcing.

## Usage Guidelines
- Use stereo headphones at conservative volume.
- Keep posture stable and jaw/shoulders relaxed.
