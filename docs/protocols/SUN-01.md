# SUN-01 - Small Universe - Beginner Orbit

## Overview
**Duration**: 18 minutes  
**Family**: SUN (Small Universe)  
**Intensity**: Low  
**Experience Level**: Beginner to Intermediate

## What This Session Is For
- Primary goal: cultivate smooth Small Universe circulation anchored in lower dantian stability.
- Protocol-specific aim: coordinate breath, attention, and orbit pathway without force.
- Best use case: seated qigong/meditation practice where subtle internal tracking is available.

## Session Methodology
Keep the practice gentle and continuous: natural breath, soft attention, and consistent return to lower dantian.

### Phase Map
- Phase 1: **Settle** (0:00-3:00) - Relax body and gather at lower dantian
- Phase 2: **Orbit Foundation** (3:00-15:00) - One gentle orbit per 6-6 breath cycle
- Phase 3: **Seal** (15:00-18:00) - Store and seal at lower dantian

## Mechanism (How It Influences State)
Breath-cycle cues map awareness through ascent/descent pathways while maintaining lower-dantian grounding.

In practical terms, the protocol combines:
- Repetition for state stabilization
- Gradual transitions to prevent abrupt overactivation
- Cue-based timing to guide attention, breath, and pacing

## Theory (Why This Design)
Integrates qigong microcosmic-orbit framing with modern self-regulation principles: smooth breath, no force, consistent closure at lower dantian.

This is a training protocol, not a one-off effect. Benefits usually accumulate with repeated practice.

- Mid-session shift: ascent/descent orbit tracking becomes smoother per breath.
