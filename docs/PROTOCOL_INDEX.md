## Safety Information

### General Precautions
- Use headphones for binaural beats
- Don't use while driving or operating machinery
- Wait 15-30 minutes after protocol before driving
- Stay hydrated
- Have a safe space to process emotions

### Contraindications
**Epilepsy**: Avoid rhythmic protocols (ARC, BODY, CREA)
**Severe Trauma**: Use RECON protocols only with therapeutic support
**Psychosis History**: Avoid META protocols, use grounding protocols
**Pregnancy**: Consult healthcare provider before intense protocols

### When to Stop
Stop immediately if you experience:
- Severe anxiety or panic
- Dissociation that feels unsafe
- Physical pain or discomfort
- Overwhelming emotions

## Getting Started

1. **Choose a protocol** based on your current need
2. **Read the full documentation** for that protocol
3. **Prepare your space**: comfortable position, headphones, no interruptions
4. **Set intention**: know why you're doing this protocol
5. **Complete the protocol**: don't stop midway unless necessary
6. **Integration time**: allow 10-15 minutes after to process
