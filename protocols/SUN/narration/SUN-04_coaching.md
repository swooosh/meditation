# SUN-04: Small Universe - 13-Point Dantian Orbit - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Small Universe 13-Point Dantian Orbit, a 30-minute circulation practice.

You may sit, stand, or lie down. Place the tongue lightly on the roof of the mouth and relax the body.

In your mind: **I am in the universe. The universe is in my body. The universe and I are combined.**

**Goal:** Coordinate inhale-receive and exhale-transfer through the full 13-point orbit.

**Breath:** Inhale 6 seconds, exhale 6 seconds. No strain.

On inhale, receive energy at the current point.
On exhale, move energy to the next point.

Let's begin.

---

## Orbit Path (Repeat Continuously)

1. Lower dantian  
2. Bladder  
3. Bottom of torso  
4. Tailbone tip  
5. Lower back  
6. Kidney area  
7. Spinal cord (neck)  
8. Base of head  
9. Top of head  
10. Third eye  
11. Throat  
12. Heart  
13. Return to lower dantian

---

## Phase 1: Settle and Connect (00:00-03:00)

**00:00** Rest awareness deep behind the navel at lower dantian.

**01:00** Visualize front and back channels connected. Feel inherited life-force and personal energy joining and brightening.

**02:30** Keep jaw, throat, and shoulders soft. Prepare to begin the orbit sequence.

## Phase 2: Guided 13-Point Orbit (03:00-18:00)

**03:00** Inhale: receive at lower dantian. Exhale: move to bladder.

**04:00** Inhale at bladder. Exhale to bottom of torso.

**05:00** Inhale at bottom. Exhale to tailbone tip.

**06:00** Inhale at tailbone. Exhale to lower back.

**07:00** Inhale at lower back. Exhale to kidney area.

**08:00** Inhale at kidney area. Exhale to spinal cord in the neck.

**09:00** Inhale at spinal cord. Exhale to base of head.

**10:00** Inhale at base of head. Exhale to top of head.

**11:00** Inhale at top of head. Exhale to third eye.

**12:00** Inhale at third eye. Exhale to throat.

**13:00** Inhale at throat. Exhale to heart.

**14:00** Inhale at heart. Exhale to lower dantian.

**15:00** Continue this exact sequence: inhale receive, exhale transfer, point by point.

**17:30** Maintain smooth continuity through the full orbit.

## Phase 3: Self Practice (18:00-27:00)

**18:00** Continue on your own at the same pace.

**21:00** Keep the pathway clear and continuous. If pressure builds in the head, return awareness to lower dantian.

**24:00** Stay relaxed and precise. No forcing.

**27:00** Begin closing.

## Phase 4: Seal (27:00-30:00)

**27:00** Rest at lower dantian. Let all orbiting soften.

**28:00** Visualize energy spinning clockwise at lower dantian like a small bright sphere.

**29:00** Let the sphere become smaller and settle deep behind the navel.

**29:30** Take three deeper breaths, staying centered at lower dantian.

**30:00** Practice complete.
