# SUN-02: Small Universe - Intermediate Orbit - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Small Universe Intermediate Orbit, a 24-minute circulation practice.

**Goal:** Refine back-channel ascent and front-channel descent with a relaxed, even mind.

**Breath:** Inhale 6 seconds (rise), exhale 8 seconds (descend). Keep it smooth and unforced.

Let's begin.

---

## Phase 1: Settle (00:00-04:00)

**00:00** Ground awareness in lower dantian. Release unnecessary tension.

**02:00** Let exhale lengthen slightly longer than inhale.

**04:00** Transition to active orbit work.

## Phase 2: Back Channel Rise (04:00-13:00)

**04:00** Inhale awareness from lower dantian up the spine toward crown.

**07:00** Keep throat soft and eyes relaxed. Do not force energy upward.

**10:00** If head pressure appears, return attention to lower dantian.

**13:00** Shift to descent emphasis.

## Phase 3: Front Channel Descent (13:00-21:00)

**13:00** Exhale awareness down forehead, throat, chest, and abdomen to lower dantian.

**16:00** Maintain one complete orbit per breath cycle.

**19:00** Keep breath quiet and continuous.

**21:00** Begin closing.

## Phase 4: Seal (21:00-24:00)

**21:00** Let orbiting dissolve into stillness at lower dantian.

**22:30** Collect and store. Hands can rest over lower dantian.

**24:00** Seal complete.
