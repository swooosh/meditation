# FSP-03: Rapid Context Switching - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Rapid Context Switching, a 15-minute protocol for cognitive flexibility.

**Goal:** Train rapid, clean transitions between different attentional contexts.

**Mechanism:** Sharp tone cues every 45 seconds signal context switches, with 12 Hz binaural beats maintaining alertness during transitions.

**Process:** Three phases. Establish baseline, practice rapid switching for 9 minutes, then stabilize the new flexibility.

Let's begin.
