# ARC-2: Gentle Activation - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Gentle Activation, a 45-minute breathwork protocol for beginners.

**Goal:** Build breathwork capacity safely through gradual intensity progression.

**Mechanism:** Tribal drum patterns guide breathing from 6 to 12 breaths per minute and back, with sub-bass frequencies supporting somatic grounding.

**Process:** Four phases. We ground at 6 BPM, ramp to 12 BPM, hold the plateau, then descend back to baseline. This is your introduction to breath-driven intensity.

Let's begin.
