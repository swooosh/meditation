# META-03: No-Self Inquiry - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to No-Self Inquiry, a 35-minute protocol for investigating the nature of self.

**Goal:** Directly investigate the question "Who am I?" and discover what remains.

**Mechanism:** Ultra-minimal 4 Hz theta binaural beats create maximum space for inquiry without distraction.

**Process:** Three phases. Ground in the inquiry, let the sense of self dissolve, then rest in what remains when there's no one there. This is advanced practice.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Ground (0-10 minutes)

**00:00** No-self inquiry. Settle into this space.

**01:30** Who am I? Not to solve, but to look through.

**03:00** Where does this "I" begin? Where does it end?

**04:30** What is this I? Search for it.

**06:00** Who is looking for the "I"?

**07:30** Rest in not-knowing.

**09:00** What remains when the questioner disappears?

## Phase 2: Dissolve (10-25 minutes)

**10:00** Where is the self? Look everywhere.

**12:00** The "I" is everywhere and nowhere.

**14:00** What is this I that thinks? Where is the thinker?

**16:00** The more you look, the more you disappear.

**18:00** Who am I? Let this burn away everything.

**20:00** Rest where seeker and sought collapse.

**22:00** Awareness aware of awareness. Never two.

**24:00** Where is the self? Everywhere and nowhere.

## Phase 3: Rest (25-35 minutes)

**25:00** Let all seeking cease. Rest as what you are.

**27:00** No one to be. Nothing to find.

**29:00** If a new "I" arises, let that dissolve too.

**31:00** Feel the freedom of no-self.

**33:00** Who is returning? What never left?

**34:00** The "I" that emerges is empty, transparent, free.

**35:00** Rest complete. The inquiry continues in life itself.
