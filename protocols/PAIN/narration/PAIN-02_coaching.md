# PAIN-02: Sensation Reframe - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Sensation Reframe, a 20-minute protocol for changing pain meaning.

**Goal:** Reinterpret pain signals by changing their cognitive and emotional meaning.

**Mechanism:** 8 Hz alpha binaural beats with harmonic pads create space for reframing the interpretation of sensation.

**Process:** Three phases. Establish baseline sensation, actively reframe its meaning, then integrate the new interpretation. This is cognitive pain management.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Baseline (0-5 minutes)

**00:00** Sensation reframe. Notice the baseline signal.

**01:00** What is the sensation? Observe precisely.

**02:00** You interpret sensation. Notice this.

**03:00** The signal is neutral. You give it meaning.

**04:00** Prepare to reframe the signal.

## Phase 2: Reframe (5-15 minutes)

**05:00** Now reframe. You interpret this signal.

**06:00** What if this sensation means something different?

**07:00** Reframe the signal. Change the meaning.

**08:00** You interpret sensation. You have this control.

**09:00** Notice how meaning changes experience.

**10:00** Reframe the signal. You control interpretation.

**11:00** What new meaning can you give this?

**12:00** You interpret sensation. Change the meaning.

**13:00** Reframe the signal. You have control.

**14:00** Notice how reframing changes the signal.

## Phase 3: Integrate (15-20 minutes)

**15:00** Now integrate. You interpret sensation.

**16:00** Notice the new meaning. You changed this.

**17:00** You have control over interpretation.

**18:00** The signal is what you make it. You control meaning.

**19:00** Notice the integration. You reframed the signal.

**20:00** Complete. You interpret sensation. You have control.
