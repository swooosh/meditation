# CREA-02: Divergent Thinking - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Divergent Thinking, a 20-minute protocol designed to expand your creative capacity.

**Goal:** Generate multiple novel ideas without judgment or constraint.

**Mechanism:** We'll use irregular audio pulses and gamma frequency overlays to disrupt habitual thought patterns, creating space for unexpected connections.

**Process:** Three phases. First, we establish a baseline awareness. Then, we actively diverge - letting ideas multiply freely without evaluation. Finally, we integrate - noticing what emerged without forcing conclusions.

Let's begin.

---

## Phase 1: Baseline (0-5 minutes)

**00:00** Welcome to divergent thinking. Find your space.

**00:30** Notice what's here right now. No need to change anything.

**01:00** What thoughts are moving through? Let them be.

**01:30** Breathe naturally. What's arising in your awareness?

**02:00** Feel into your creative state. No judgment needed.

**02:30** What wants to emerge? Don't grasp for it.

**03:00** Simply observe. What patterns do you notice?

**03:30** Let your mind wander freely. No destination required.

**04:00** What's the quality of your attention right now?

**04:30** Prepare to let thinking become more fluid.

## Phase 2: Diverge (5-15 minutes)

**05:00** Now let your mind expand. Release all boundaries.

**05:30** What if there were no wrong answers? Explore that space.

**06:00** Let ideas flow without editing. Messy is perfect.

**06:30** What's the wildest possibility? Don't dismiss anything.

**07:00** Follow tangents. Where do they lead?

**07:30** What connections are forming? Let them be loose.

**08:00** Multiply your options. More, not better.

**08:30** What would happen if you removed all constraints?

**09:00** Let contradictions coexist. Don't resolve them yet.

**09:30** What's emerging from the chaos? Just notice.

**10:00** Expand further. What else is possible?

**10:30** Let your thinking become even more fluid.

**11:00** What patterns want to break? Let them.

**11:30** Generate without evaluating. Pure creation.

**12:00** What's arising that surprises you?

**12:30** Follow the energy of curiosity wherever it goes.

**13:00** Let ideas cross-pollinate freely.

**13:30** What wants to be explored? Don't grasp.

**14:00** Notice the abundance of possibilities.

**14:30** Prepare to gently gather what's emerged.

## Phase 3: Integrate (15-20 minutes)

**15:00** Begin to sense what wants to stay. No forcing.

**15:30** What themes are naturally arising?

**16:00** Let connections form organically. Don't push.

**16:30** What feels most alive from all that emerged?

**17:00** Notice patterns without collapsing them too quickly.

**17:30** What wants to be remembered? Let it settle.

**18:00** Feel the creative energy integrating.

**18:30** What did you discover in the divergence?

**19:00** Let it all be messy and complete at once.

**19:30** Carry this expansive quality with you.

**20:00** Session complete.
