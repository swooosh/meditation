# CREA-03: Pattern Disruption - Coaching Narration

## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Pattern Disruption, a 25-minute protocol for breaking creative blocks.

**Goal:** Disrupt habitual patterns to allow new creative solutions to emerge.

**Mechanism:** White noise with irregular pulses and gamma bursts actively interrupt your default thinking patterns.

**Process:** Three phases. Notice your patterns, let them break apart, then observe what emerges from the disruption. This is creative destruction.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Baseline (0-5 minutes)

**00:00** Pattern disruption. Notice your patterns.

**01:00** What patterns are you running? Just notice.

**02:00** How do you usually think? Feel into that.

**03:00** What's familiar? What's automatic?

**04:00** Prepare to let patterns break.

## Phase 2: Disrupt (5-20 minutes)

**05:00** Let it break. Whatever pattern you're holding.

**06:00** Don't fix it. Let the disruption happen.

**07:30** What emerges when patterns break?

**09:00** Let it break. Your usual way.

**10:30** What wants to emerge? Don't hold on.

**12:00** Let it break completely. What emerges?

**13:30** Don't hold on. Let disruption happen.

**15:00** Let it break. What emerges now?

**16:30** Notice what's trying to come through.

**18:00** Don't hold on. Let it be fluid.

**19:30** One more wave. Let it break.

## Phase 3: Emerge (20-25 minutes)

**20:00** Now let something new emerge.

**21:00** What's different? What emerged?

**22:00** Don't capture it. Let it emerge.

**23:00** What emerges when you trust?

**24:00** Notice what shifted.

**24:30** What emerges? Don't hold on.

**25:00** Let it break. What emerges? Complete.
