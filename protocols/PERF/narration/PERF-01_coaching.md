## Introduction (Record this first)

Welcome to Pre-Mission Activation, a 12-minute protocol for optimal performance preparation.

**Goal:** Achieve peak readiness state before high-stakes performance.

**Mechanism:** Ramping beta-range binaural beats with sharp pulse cues progressively activate your nervous system to performance-ready state.

**Process:** Three phases. Baseline activation, ramp to peak alertness, then stabilize at optimal arousal. Use this 15 minutes before important events.

Let's begin.

---


## Phase 1: Ground (0:00 - 3:00)

**00:00**
Sit upright. Spine straight. Eyes closed. Breathe 4 in, 6 out.

**01:00**
You're preparing for performance. This is deliberate calm.

**02:00**
Feel your feet on the ground. You're stable.

---

## Phase 2: Sharpen (3:00 - 9:00)

**03:00**
Narrow your attention. Pick a single point of focus.

**04:30**
Your mind is sharpening. Let it.

**06:00**
Notice clarity increasing. This is your ready state.

**07:30**
Stay with the focus point. Distractions fall away.

---

## Phase 3: Lock (9:00 - 12:00)

**09:00**
You're locked in. Calm and alert.

**10:00**
This is your baseline for action. Remember this feeling.

**11:00**
When you open your eyes, you'll carry this state forward.

**11:45**
Ready.
