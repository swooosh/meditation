# Protocol Family Taxonomy

## Family Identities

### 1. ATTN (Attention Architecture)
**Audio Identity**
- Beds: Pink/brown noise, moderate drift (0.008-0.014 Hz)
- Cues: Clean sine tones, return bells
- Binaural: Precise frequency control, alpha-beta range
- Signature: 220-293-440 Hz triad

**Narration Tone**
- Technical, instructional
- Minimal emotional content
- Clear phase transitions
- "Notice", "Observe", "Return to"

**Default Presets**
- Beginner: Shorter phases, more cues
- Standard: Balanced
- Advanced: Longer phases, minimal guidance

**Protocols**
- FSP-01: Focused State Protocol (31m)
- FSP-02: Distributed Attention (25m) [NEW]
- FSP-03: Rapid Context Switching (15m) [NEW]

### 2. ARC (Breath-Driven Intensity)
**Audio Identity**
- Beds: Tribal percussion, skin-forward drums
- Cues: Rhythmic, breath-coupled
- Sub: 40-60 Hz, breath-rate modulated
- Room: Wide early reflections
- Signature: 174-220-293 Hz triad

**Narration Tone**
- Directive, commanding
- Physical language

**Default Presets**

- Advanced: 125% intensity, extended plateau

## Family Relationships

**Activation Axis**
- Low: SLEEP, META
- Medium: ATTN, RECON, PAIN
- High: ARC, BODY, SENSE

**Attention Axis**
- Narrow: ATTN, PERF, FLOW
- Wide: SENSE, CREA
- Dissolved: META

**Emotional Axis**
- Neutral: ATTN, PERF, FLOW
- Processing: RECON, BODY
- Relational: SOC

**Somatic Axis**
- Minimal: ATTN, META, CREA
- Moderate: SENSE, RECON
- Intense: ARC, BODY, PAIN
